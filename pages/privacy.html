Many Paths privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Many Paths is a free Android app that lists recovery meetings (AA, NA, Refuge Recovery, Recovery Dharma, CMA, SMART, Wellbriety and others) near a place you choose. It is made by one person, Benyahman Lunn, with no company, investors or advertisers behind it. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any fellowship.

In short: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app has no server of its own and the developer never receives anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Your saved meetings, meetings you add yourself, the place you search near, your filters and settings are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else. If Android's own backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include them in your device backup with Google, the same as other apps' settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Location

The app asks for approximate location, and reads it only when you tap "Use my current location" (or "Use where I am now" while adding a meeting). It never reads your location in the background. You can type a city or ZIP code instead and never grant it.

  • Distances to meetings are worked out on your phone.
  • To find NA meetings, the app sends the NA meeting database (BMLT, run by NA volunteers) a point rounded to about 7 miles, so it can return meetings in your area. It does not send your exact location.
  • To show the name of your town, and to find a city you type, the app uses Android's built-in place lookup, which your phone's maker or Google provides.

Meeting lists

The app downloads public meeting lists from a daily copy on GitHub Pages (benyahmanlunndev.github.io) and, when there is no copy, straight from each group's website. Like any website visit, those servers can see your phone's internet address and that the request came from Many Paths. They do not receive your location, your saved meetings or anything that identifies you. See GitHub's privacy statement for how GitHub handles visits.

Sharing, calls and links

When you share a meeting or a week's list, call a phone line, get directions or open a meeting's website, the app hands that to the app you pick (your messages, phone, maps or browser app). What happens next is up to that app.

Children

Many Paths is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub, or use the email address on the app's Google Play page.
